# Shopify playbook

Use with the generic playbook when the storefront, checkout, app or pixel evidence indicates Shopify. Sources: [Customer Privacy API](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/customer-privacy), [pixel privacy](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/web-pixels-api/pixel-privacy), [app privacy compliance](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/compliance/privacy-law-compliance). Accessed 2026-11-18; recheck before use. Shopify functionality and configuration do not establish a legal ground or compliance.

| ID | Evidence collection | False positive / limit | Fix target; owner; acceptance and retest |
|---|---|---|---|
| SHP-01 | Public: inspect sampled storefront notices and choices. Authorized: inspect customer privacy configuration, applicable regions, theme code, consent state and each pixel's privacy settings. | A platform setting or banner does not prove all custom scripts honor choices; a script URL does not prove transfer. | Merchant developer and privacy: align purposes, notices and settings; in authorized staging observe synthetic accept/reject/withdraw flows and downstream events. |
| SHP-02 | Authorized: inventory apps, custom pixels and other processors, purposes and agreements; compare storefront disclosure. | Installed app is not necessarily activated on every journey; public script list is incomplete. | Merchant operations: reconcile actual app data flows and processor terms; trace a synthetic event and verify inventory. |
| SHP-03 | Authorized: trace rights requests through merchant, apps, exports and retention/holds. For a Shopify app, inspect applicable mandatory compliance webhooks and handler outcomes. | Shopify's mandatory app webhook requirements are not a requirement for every merchant to implement webhooks. A received webhook alone is not completion. | Merchant privacy and app developer: ensure scoped requests reach each recipient with auditable completion or documented exception; exercise synthetic requests. |
| SHP-04 | Authorized: inspect staff access, app scopes, incident recovery, data schedules and child audience/guardian flow if relevant. | App permissions alone do not establish misuse; unknown audience is not evidence of child processing. | Merchant security and product: remove unjustified access and fill evidenced process gaps; review permissions and test a staged relevant journey. |

## SHP-00 — merchant self-check

Use an authorised, read-only admin session or ask the merchant for redacted screenshots. These settings locate evidence; they do not independently prove deployment or DPDP compliance. Do not change settings or trigger customer data requests during an ordinary audit.

| Where to look | Record and assess | Could not check |
|---|---|---|
| Settings > Customer privacy | Banner regions and privacy-policy configuration; compare the actual Indian visitor journey and disclosed purposes. A configured banner alone is not a pass, and its absence alone is not a DPDP violation. | Actual region/session behaviour or unavailable admin access |
| Settings > Customer events | App/custom pixel inventory and each pixel's privacy permissions; compare declared purposes with observed events before and after a choice. | Server-side recipients, undocumented app injections or uncaptured network activity |
| Settings > Checkout > Marketing opt-in | Email/SMS choices, region settings and default selection. A preselected choice warrants a consent-quality finding where affirmative consent is the claimed ground. | Actual checkout state if only a configuration screenshot exists |
| Settings > Policies / Customer privacy | Read the published notice against actual data, purposes, contact and rights routes. A generated policy needs business-specific review. | Completeness of the business's processor/retention inventory |
| Customers > selected profile > More actions | Locate the request-data and erasure workflow using documentation or a synthetic test profile. Record identity checks, responsibility and exceptions. | Completion across apps, exports, backups or other recipients without separate evidence |
| Storefront newsletter/footer, Forms, Inbox and installed apps | Trace each visible collection surface and any separate marketing choice, including WhatsApp/COD and third-party phone/OTP checkout flows if present. Treat separate recipients as separate flows. | A source/app reference without runtime or configuration evidence |
| Customer accounts configuration and the actual account journey | Record the account type and available rights/contact routes; check the journey users actually receive. A missing self-service deletion button alone does not establish absence of an operational erasure route. | Account-only screens and backend request completion without authorised access |

Merchant procedure references checked 18 November 2026: [customer privacy settings](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/privacy-and-security/privacy/customer-privacy-settings/privacy-settings), [custom pixel settings](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/pixels/custom-pixels/manage), [checkout options](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/checkout-settings/checkout-form-options), [customer data requests](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/privacy-and-security/privacy/processing-customer-data-requests). Menu labels and permissions can vary; record the observed UI. Shopify's data-request action does not establish erasure in every separately connected company. Review platform and app roles/destinations against the legal reference, including Rule 15 where applicable, without assuming all foreign processing is prohibited.

## Developer procedures

**SHP-01 — theme and pixel controls.** Request the deployed theme version, relevant theme scripts, region settings, app-pixel extension configuration and custom-pixel privacy settings. Map each optional event to its purpose and actual recipient. In theme scripts inspect the initial result of `window.Shopify.customerPrivacy.analyticsProcessingAllowed()` or `marketingAllowed()` as appropriate and the `visitorConsentCollected` document event for subsequent changes; the event fires on change, so a listener alone does not establish initial state. Inspect script initialization and all event sends, including scripts inserted by apps. In an app pixel, inspect `[customer_privacy]` in `shopify.extension.toml`; the pixel manager gates loading on all declared required permissions. Custom-pixel requirements are configured in its UI. Inside pixel sandboxes, examine `init.customerPrivacy` and `customerPrivacy.subscribe('visitorConsentCollected', ...)`, rather than assuming the theme API is the same interface.

Merchant developer: correct the relevant theme script or pixel declarations and verify an authorized synthetic staging session before choice, after rejection, after acceptance and after withdrawal, including reload and any in-page change. Capture permission state, the relevant network requests and configured region. A referenced script does not itself prove a request; a successful staged test does not establish deployment. Compare notice text and actual purposes with privacy owner before changing configurations.

**SHP-02 — vendor mapping.** Request a redacted installed-app and custom-pixel inventory, their scopes, purpose, actual recipients and contract owners. Compare the list to the sampled theme and network trace; ask the app owner to explain data sent server-side. Merchant operations reconcile material discrepancies in disclosures and contracts. Acceptance: synthetic events are traceable to mapped recipients, with unresolved recipients recorded as unknown.

**SHP-03 — rights and app handlers.** Trace a synthetic request through the merchant process, relevant apps, exports and downstream retention exceptions. For an app distributed through the Shopify App Store, review its `shopify.app.toml` compliance subscriptions and handlers for `customers/data_request`, `customers/redact` and `shop/redact`; Shopify identifies these as mandatory topics for those apps, not a requirement for every merchant to build webhooks. Ask for authenticated-handler, queue/retry and completion evidence, including legal-hold handling. A successful delivery acknowledgment is not proof of completed action. The app developer may use Shopify's documented test delivery in an authorized test app; a manually triggered delivery does not test subscription configuration. Acceptance: synthetic data is accounted for across mapped systems, completion or exception recorded, and the subscription independently verified.

**SHP-04 — access and conditional children.** Request redacted staff roles, app scopes and incident/recovery exercise evidence; product owner supplies audience evidence and, where relevant, staged guardian-flow tests. Review actual permissions and behavior rather than assuming exposure from an app scope. Retest changed roles and applicable flows without accessing real customer records.
