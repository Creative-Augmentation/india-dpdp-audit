## Developer procedures

**WOO-01 — notices and collection.** Request a redacted screenshot or export of **WooCommerce > Settings > Accounts & Privacy**, the active theme and checkout/registration extension list. In its Privacy policy section compare registration and checkout notice text and policy link to the rendered synthetic staging journeys; the policy page is selected under WordPress **Settings > Privacy**. Trace custom fields, opt-in state and their downstream uses in theme and plugin code. Store developer updates the actual rendering and processing path, then retests each relevant form and choice in staging. Settings alone do not prove an overridden theme or extension renders them.

**WOO-02 — recipients.** Inventory payment, analytics, shipping, marketing and custom extensions, including any external APIs and where they retain records. Request a redacted inventory and contracts, then trace a synthetic staged order or event. Store operations reconcile observed recipients and disclosures; a plugin name is a lead, not proof of a particular transfer.

**WOO-03 — retention and rights.** In **WooCommerce > Settings > Accounts & Privacy > Personal data retention**, inspect each relevant period and account erasure option alongside the approved schedule. WooCommerce documents blank retention fields as indefinite retention for that option; when configured, cleanup runs daily, with different outcomes such as trashing certain unpaid orders versus anonymizing completed or refunded orders. Do not equate a cleanup setting, trash, anonymization and deletion. Review scheduled-job evidence and holds before determining an outcome.

In WordPress **Tools > Export Personal Data** and **Tools > Erase Personal Data**, review a synthetic request and confirmation flow in authorized staging. Inspect custom plugins for registration with `wp_privacy_personal_data_exporters` and `wp_privacy_personal_data_erasers`, their callbacks, pagination and success/retained-item results. Compare callback coverage against custom tables, metadata, files, external plugin services, exports and backups; hooks alone do not cover external records automatically. Plugin developer adds missing coverage or documented handoff; privacy operations track justified retained records and their review date. Acceptance: synthetic export and erasure reach each mapped store or produce a recorded exception, and an independent check verifies the result. Do not request a real customer export. See [WordPress exporter hook](https://developer.wordpress.org/reference/hooks/wp_privacy_personal_data_exporters/) and [eraser guide](https://developer.wordpress.org/plugins/privacy/adding-the-personal-data-eraser-to-your-plugin/).

**WOO-04 — access and children.** Request redacted admin roles, plugin update process, recovery exercise and incident runbook. Review child audience evidence and relevant guardian flow only if applicable. Security and product owners address demonstrated gaps and retest access and recovery in authorized staging; a plugin version alone is not proof of exploitation.
