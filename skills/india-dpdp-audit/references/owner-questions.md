# Bounded owner questions

Ask only questions that materially change the scoped findings and are not answered by available evidence. Public-only audits can proceed with unknowns. Request redacted configuration, synthetic test results or policy excerpts, not customer exports, credentials or production records. Record the responder, date, scope and whether evidence corroborates an answer.

1. Which entity, domains, products and Indian users or processing are in scope? Are children a target audience or otherwise known to be processed? What evidence supports the answer, and are any exemptions relevant?
2. For the sampled journeys, what personal data, purposes and asserted grounds apply? Which notice and consent versions were deployed, and how do withdrawal and any purpose changes propagate?
3. Which vendors and subprocessors receive sampled data, for which purposes and locations? Can you share a redacted inventory and relevant contractual controls?
4. How are rights, grievances and applicable nominations received, identity-checked and completed across vendors? Can you share a synthetic exercise or redacted process evidence?
5. What are the schedules, legal holds and deletion mechanisms for sampled stores, backups and recipients? Can you provide a synthetic deletion test including descendants and exceptions?
6. Who controls access, incident response and recovery? Can you share scoped policy/configuration evidence and results of an authorized staging exercise?
7. Is the entity notified as a Significant Data Fiduciary or subject to a sector-specific obligation? What notification or counsel analysis supports that conclusion?

Legitimate unknowns include inaccessible admin settings, unverified deployment versions, incomplete vendor inventories and pending legal applicability analysis. Mark each unknown and next evidence owner; do not turn missing proof into an assumed failure.
