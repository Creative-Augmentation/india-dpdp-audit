# Legal starting points

Source review: 2026-11-18. The Act, commencement notification, final Rules, December corrigenda, October order, PIB May 2026 statement and the SPDI Gazette collection were inspected during this review. A follow-up after independent review also retrieved the 2011 PIB clarification through an alternate official URL and identified the January 2026 upload below. The MeitY framework index yielded no readable text and India Code timed out, so this is not an exhaustive confirmation that no later notification exists.

Reverify at every audit and before regulatory phase changes. Distinguish the printed Gazette date from digital publication and website upload. 13 May 2027 remains the conservative preparation date corroborated by the PIB statement; some official summaries compute 14 May. Retain the underlying publication-date uncertainty when giving an exact legal deadline. This is a source map, not legal advice or a legal opinion.

## Primary sources

- [DPDP Act 2023, official copy](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf): sections 3–7 scope/grounds/notice/consent, 8 fiduciary duties, 9 children/guardians, 10 SDF, 11–14 rights, 16 transfers, 17 exemptions. The original Act alone does not establish commencement.
- [G.S.R. 843(E), commencement](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf), Gazette 13 November 2025: immediate institutional provisions; sections 6(9) and 27(1)(d) after one year; most substantive processing obligations after eighteen months. Section 44(2), relevant to IT Act amendments, is in the eighteen-month group.
- [Final DPDP Rules 2025, G.S.R. 846(E)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf), Gazette 13 November 2025. English begins PDF page 24. Rule 1 phases commencement: rules 1, 2, 17–21 immediately; rule 4 after one year; rules 3, 5–16, 22, 23 after eighteen months. Consult the exact rule text and schedules for conditional thresholds and exceptions.
- [G.S.R. 892(E), corrigenda](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/12/3c7ebbae0e5456f493f486e6845df86b.pdf), dated 10 December 2025, Gazette 11 December 2025: includes correction of commencement wording to publication “in the Official Gazette.” Apply corrections when reading the Rules.
- [PIB, 16 May 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2261823): PIB reports the MeitY Secretary, at an 11 May 2026 workshop, referring to the DPDP Act "becoming fully enforceable from 13 May 2027". Use this as corroboration of the Gazette timeline, not as a substitute for notification text. Some government website summaries use 14 November 2025 as the notification/upload date; retain that distinction.
- [SPDI Rules 2011, G.S.R. 313(E), government-hosted Gazette collection](https://emb.mes.gov.in/Common/help/gsr3_10511.pdf) (SPDI begins PDF page 7; the collection starts with a different instrument) and [PIB clarification, 24 August 2011](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=74990) (retrieved 2026-11-18): assess present applicability separately, including Rule 4 privacy policy and contractual processing under Rules 5/6. Do not treat all ordinary contact information as SPDI or assume absence of SPDI resolves every Rule 4 question. IT Act section 43A remains relevant until DPDP section 44(2) commences. The older [MeitY PDF route](https://www.meity.gov.in/writereaddata/files/GSR313E_10511%281%29_0.pdf) returned 404 in the follow-up; the government-hosted Gazette is retained explicitly as a fallback, not silently represented as a MeitY copy.
- [January 2026 MeitY upload](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/01/a49c50414777afc2af0b8d59dafacd60.pdf): a consultation-feedback status document about the January 2025 draft, not consolidated final Rules. Its upload path and title do not establish legislative status; do not apply its draft numbering to the final Rules.

As of 18 November 2026, the one-year phase date for Rule 4 (Consent Manager registration) and sections 6(9) and 27(1)(d) has passed under both the 13 and 14 November readings. This does not establish registration of any particular organisation or portal functionality. Most website-facing duties remain **readiness requirements** under the instruments reviewed, with the substantive phase due May 2027; sections 6(7)-(8) sit in that later group. Check subsequent notifications before classifying a current violation.

Do not derive changed deadlines from unsourced secondary reports about a shortened transition. A deadline change needs the relevant primary notification. Record an inaccessible source or unresolved change explicitly rather than treating a failed search as proof that nothing changed.

## October 2026 Removal of Difficulties Order

[Official S.O. 5458(E)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/10/daf495a7b5798a3dfa72a944b51fc176.pdf), order dated 5 October 2026, Gazette issue dated 6 October, digitally published 7 October: verified English text on PDF pages 2–3. It clarifies section 9(1)'s parallel reference to a child or a person with disability who has a lawful guardian, and changes section 10(2)(c)(ii) to periodic data audit. It contains no change to the substantive commencement timetable. Apply these corrections rather than treating the supplied 2023 Act PDF as consolidated current law.

## Interpretation checks

- Section 5/Rule 3 consent notice is purpose-specific. Section 5(3) requires an option to access the notice in English or an Eighth Schedule language; assess that option in the actual journey. A generic policy alone may not satisfy the notice requirement. These provisions remain in the May 2027 phase under the reviewed instruments.
- Section 8(9) permits an appropriate authorised contact where a statutory DPO is not required. Section 10 additional duties depend on SDF designation.
- Sections 11–14 and Rule 14 cover distinct rights. Rule 14(3)'s maximum ninety-day period concerns the published grievance-redressal response period and supporting measures. Do not generalise it into a universal deadline for every access/erasure request from a backgrounder. This is a May 2027 readiness check under the reviewed instruments.
- Rule 6 safeguards and Rule 7 breach notifications need internal evidence. An HTTPS padlock proves neither.
- Rule 7 distinguishes initial notifications without delay from subsequent detailed Board reporting within 72 hours, or a longer period the Board permits on written request. Do not convert that later report deadline into a general waiting period. Rule 7 remains in the later commencement phase; assess any separate currently applicable CERT-In or sector duty on its own source and trigger.
- Rule 8 and schedules require careful retention analysis; avoid applying category-specific inactivity periods to every site or treating one-year security retention as permission for indefinite general retention.
- Section 9 and Rules 10–12 require applicability and exemption checks. Section 9(3) restricts child tracking, behavioural monitoring and child-directed targeted advertising, subject to applicable exceptions. General business positioning is not proof that no children use a service. The reviewed commencement is May 2027.
- Section 16, Rule 15's foreign-state availability orders, conditional Rule 13(4) restrictions for SDFs and separate sector rules need distinct checks. Foreign hosting alone does not establish a prohibited transfer. Rule 15 is in the May 2027 group; determine which orders and sector rules actually apply.
- GDPR/ePrivacy requirements, cookie-banner conventions and general security hardening are not automatically Indian statutory requirements. Label any separately applicable law or optional advice.
